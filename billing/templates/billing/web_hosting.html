PLANNING YOUR WEBSITE

A few useful answers.

Do I need a domain name?

A domain gives visitors an address for your website. Once hosting is ready, its DNS records must point to the hosting destination. Domain registration is separate unless your chosen package explicitly includes it.

Will my application work on shared hosting?

Check its runtime, database and background-process requirements against the package features before ordering. Custom server software may require a VPS.
