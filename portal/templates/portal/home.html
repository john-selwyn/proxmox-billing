GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Which hosting option should I choose?

Web hosting suits a conventional website without custom server requirements. A VPS gives you a separate server environment for applications that need more configuration.

Can I see VPS prices without an account?

Yes. Browse the VPS plans first. You will need to sign in before selecting a billing cycle and placing an order.

Are web-hosting packages available?

Yes. The current Starter, Business and Pro packages are shown from the Hestia catalog. Online web-hosting checkout is the next step in the verified payment flow.

Where do I manage existing orders?

Sign in to your customer dashboard to see your orders, invoices and account details.
